What costs money?

Nothing you need in order to split a bill. Groups, members, manual expenses, 154 currencies, guests, debt reminders and offline use are free, and there are no ads. AI input is free up to 5 voice entries and 3 receipt scans a month. Ceru Pro costs $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US App Store, and prices may differ by country. It makes voice input and receipt scanning unlimited and adds analytics, group budgets with alerts, and export to PDF and Excel.

How does voice input work without internet?

It doesn't. Voice and scanning send the audio or photo to AI services, so they need a connection. Speech is transcribed by OpenAI Whisper and understood by Google Gemini. Manual entry works fully offline, and everything syncs once you are back online.

Do you sell my data?

No. There are no ads, we don't sell data, and your expenses are visible only to the members of your group.

What if AI gets the receipt wrong?

You can edit any field before you save.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Pro exports your expenses, settlements and balances as PDF or Excel.

Can I bring my data from Splitwise?

Yes. In Splitwise, open the group and choose Export as spreadsheet, then pick that file when you create a group in Ceru.

Do my friends need the app?

No. Add them as guests by name. If they join later by link, they keep their history.

Is there an Android version?

Android is in closed testing. Write to us and we will send you a build.
